Sale Order Rounding

Apply cash rounding directly on Odoo Sale Orders, display rounding in totals, and automatically pass the rounding configuration to customer invoices.

Cash Rounding for Odoo Sales Orders
Select a cash rounding method on the sale order, show the rounding amount below tax totals, and carry the same rounding setup to the generated invoice automatically.

Key Highlights

💰 Cash Rounding on Sale Orders
Add a Cash Rounding field directly on the Sale Order and select the required rounding configuration before confirming the order.
🧾 Rounding Display in Totals
Show the rounding amount directly below the tax totals section so users can clearly see the final rounded sales value.
🔁 Invoice Rounding Carry Forward
Automatically pass the selected Sale Order cash rounding method to the generated customer invoice for consistent accounting behavior.
📊 Rounded Totals Accuracy
Compute rounding differences and rounded totals based on the selected cash rounding configuration to keep pricing transparent.
⚙️ Works with Odoo Cash Rounding
Reuse standard Odoo cash rounding configurations from Accounting and apply them directly in the Sales workflow.
🚀 Better Pre-Invoice Price Visibility
Let sales teams view the exact rounding impact at quotation and order level before invoice creation, reducing billing surprises.
